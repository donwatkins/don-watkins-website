All Things Open 2026 · Raleigh, North Carolina

Eight Hours of Vibe Coding

At All Things Open 2026, I spent eight hours in a vibe coding session exploring how AI-assisted development could help me turn an idea into a website.

My years in education and open source shaped the experience. I brought curiosity, questions, and a willingness to experiment. The result was the beginning of this personal website.

Vibe coding, to me, isn't only about asking AI to write code. It's about evaluating what it creates, learning from the results, and continuing to improve the work.

The most important technology skill isn't knowing everything. It's being willing to learn something new.
